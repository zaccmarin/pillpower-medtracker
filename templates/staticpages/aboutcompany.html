About PillPower

PillPower is dedicated to improving your health and well-being by providing timely reminders and notifications for your medication. Our mission is to ensure you never miss a dose and always take the correct medication at the right time.

- Background -

PillPower.co.uk is a passion project, aimed to improve how people take medication. Looking at a wide audience, from young or old, we want to make sure that we offer an easy-to-use and effective user interface. Functionality is key, and using nhs integration offers a trusted standard to the service we offer.

Up-To-Date Information

PillPower utilises NHS database information to pull data from. When provided information from PillPower, you can have peace of mind that it will be the most up-to-date and trusted advice.

It's all you.

PillPower aims to be straightforward and easy to use. Users are able to add their own medication straight onto their PillPower account, and have PillPower sort out the rest.
